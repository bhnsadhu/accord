# Homepage surface brief

Scope: `/` (homepage), the first surface of the Blotter world. Mode: Persuade.
Audience: fragrance enthusiasts first (approved); recruiters judge it as a product.
Action: search the catalog or scan a bottle, in working form, in the first viewport.
Proof: real catalog names, real recommendation evidence. No invented ratings, prices, users.
Constraints: no catalog images; scanner gated until Modal spend limit verified (Scan shows honest availability).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every fragrance is a smelling strip and Accord is the evaluation bench. Refuses the luxury-retail hero (bottle photo, serif, shop button) and the database grid.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral bench grey ground, blotter-paper white strips with die-cut tapered tips, oil stains in one fixed hue per Edwards wheel family with a darker tide line; graphite ink; Schibsted Grotesk for all text, Geist Mono only for ids, counts and match numbers; paper drop shadows; no glass, no decorative gradients beyond the stain.

STORY: The enthusiast sees that Accord reads 24,642 fragrances by note and accord, trusts suggestions because each shows its evidence, and searches or scans at once.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left ~40%: 84px headline, lede, working search field with Scan button beside it. Right: fan of 11 real catalog strips rising from below the frame. One recommendation strip laid across the bench lower right with match and evidence. Family legend lower left. Signature interaction: selecting a strip lifts it from the fan and its stain saturates as it locks; laid strips form a retraceable trail. Motion: paper physics, exponential ease-out 500 to 700 ms, instant under reduced motion.

FORM: Smelling strips (mouillettes), grounded candidate 3 of 7. seed:53633257f486edba

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
